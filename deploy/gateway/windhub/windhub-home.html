风合智联 WindHub 多模态大模型智能体服务平台，集合国内主流已备案大模型能力。用户只需与一个智能体对话，即可统一调用文本、图片、视频等多模态大模型服务，并根据业务需求灵活选择适合的模型、能力与定价方案。通过简化模型接入与智能体应用流程，WindHub 帮助企业与组织提升工作效率、降低应用成本，实现降本增效。WindHub is a multimodal large-model agent service platform that brings together capabilities from leading registered models in China. Users can talk to one agent to call text, image, and video model services in one place, then choose models, capabilities, and pricing for their needs. By simplifying model integration and agent application workflows, WindHub helps enterprises and organizations improve efficiency, reduce application costs, and achieve more with less.
